# Legacy AMOS workspace archive

This tag preserves current local code, tests, configuration, papers and governance, including work that was untracked at the published HEAD. It is historical, unvalidated material; apparent PASS/status/results do not confer research admission.

Raw datasets, bulk result directories, compiled builds, caches and chat/data export files are excluded. Their local originals remain in the legacy folder; full hashes and dispositions are recorded in the new main audit inventory. The separate published-head tag preserves exactly what was previously pushed. No source acquisition or human approval is inferred.
